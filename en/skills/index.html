Why skills exist

Markets are not short of opinions. They are short of filters. You have a model that tells a story, a channel that dumps ten tickers, a sheet that «should» size the trade. Then the entry leaves naked. Then the stop is «later». Then the account knows.

Helmsman reverses the order. First the scan walks the liquid universe. Then each skill answers with a bool: yes or no. Only if yes does a preview appear with size and stop. Only if you confirm does the order reach the broker — already covered.

We are not selling «five magic strategies». We are selling five honest vetoes. If the day is empty, the day is empty. FOMO is not a feature.

Live catalog

Five skills. Long-only. No size invented in chat.

Each skill has a horizon, an entry logic, a stop and a target. Size stays with the risk engine on your account. The LLM does not write it. You see it before the yes.

01 multi-day · GTC

ATR-BREAKOUT

Trend + momentum

Not a textbook high-of-range breakout. The sober thesis: price above the primary average, RSI in a healthy zone, primary trend not against you. Stop from ATR. Target at 1:2 R:R. If the name bounces inside a downtrend, the skill says no — even if the chat «feels it hard».

  • Enters when last above EMA20, RSI between 50 and 70, last above SMA200
  • Stop 2× ATR(14)
  • Target 2× the stop
  • Universe full liquid book
02 short-term · GTC

CONNORS-MR

Mean reversion · buy the dip

When the tape is still above the medium average but ultra-short RSI has flushed, the skill hunts the bounce — not a knife with no floor. Wide stop on purpose: mean reversion hates tight stops. Target on the bounce, not «double in a week».

  • Enters when last above SMA50 and RSI(2) under 10
  • Stop fixed 5%
  • Target fixed 4%
  • Universe full liquid book
03 momentum · GTC

VOL-SQUEEZE

Compression · long bias

Bollinger inside Keltner: the market is holding its breath. Helmsman does not wait for the screenshot «fire». It enters during compression if the MACD histogram is already positive. Tight stop: if the thesis dies, it dies early. Wider target: catch the expansion when it comes.

  • Enters when squeeze ON and MACD hist > 0
  • Stop 1.5%
  • Target 4.5% (≈ 1:3)
  • Universe full liquid book
04 swing · GTC

QUALITY-DIP

Contrarian · oversold megacaps

The opposite of the trend skill. Buys a quality name deeply oversold, even under the long average. Not «any name on sale»: a curated whitelist — fundamentals do not come from OHLCV. Wide stop, small size: falling knife with an institutional floor, not a lottery ticket.

  • Enters when quality whitelist, RSI(14) < 30, last under SMA200
  • Stop 8%
  • Target 12% (≈ 1.5 R)
  • Universe quality megacap / blue chip only
05 swing · GTC

AVWAP-RETEST

Institutional setup · reclaim + retest

Anchored VWAP from the quarter’s low: the average price paid by those who accumulated from the base. If the name reclaimed that line and is retesting from above — without already running too far — the skill lights. It works where institutional flow dominates. On thin names it does not: hence the ultra-liquid whitelist only.

  • Enters when above daily AVWAP, retest within +4%, real base (≥8% below)
  • Stop just under the line (clamp 3–10%)
  • Target +2σ band, or 2× SL if R:R fails
  • Universe ultra-liquid ETFs and megacaps

How it enters your day

1. Scan. Helmsman walks the universe. For each name, skills answer applicable / not. Reasons in the open. No padding.

2. Preview. Only where a skill said yes does a line appear: symbol, side, size, stop, target. No order has left. If size moved or the rule died, it stops.

3. Your yes. Without a human confirm there is no order. Helmsman is middleware, not captain.

4. Covered entry. The order goes to the broker you connect — OTO or bracket — already with the stop. Then the position is your discipline.

Validation

On reference markets. Not on a vanity universe.

A skill does not enter the catalog because it «sounds good in chat». It enters if it makes sense on the liquid book Helmsman actually scans: megacaps, blue chips, ETFs where the spread does not humiliate you and the exit exists. Live rules were built and validated on those reference markets — the same tickers in the universe, not a vanity dataset.

We are not selling a 90-slide backtest with brochure CAGR. We are selling operational honesty: a bool on the tape, numbers from the engine, your yes, execution at your broker. Past performance does not predict the future. Risk stays yours. Helmsman stays the bridge.
