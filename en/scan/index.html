Three blips, not fifty alerts

The radar is not a screensaver. It is the right metaphor: it turns on liquid names and lights only what deserves a line. A name under the rule’s average stays dark. Data older than the session threshold: skipped, the rest proceed. A dead weekend tape does not become a «signal» to keep you awake.

The live rules — ATR-BREAKOUT, CONNORS-MR, VOL-SQUEEZE, QUALITY-DIP, AVWAP-RETEST — watch the market, not your account. The account arrives later, when size is computed. The scan does not propose a long because «you have buying power». It proposes one if the tape says yes. The book: 52 liquid tickers.

The tape that earns a line

Candles that move, a spread that does not humiliate you, volume that exists. The scan is an honest pass: applicable / not applicable, reasons in the open. WMT under the SMA does not become a trade because «Walmart is solid». It becomes a skip. That honesty is the product.

What a scan is not

Not a signal channel. Not an influencer ranking. Not a 90-slide backtest. Not a feed that must «find something» every morning to justify a subscription.

If the day is empty, the day is empty. Helmsman prefers three true proposals to a list that keeps you glued. FOMO is not a feature. It is how accounts empty with a smile.

From the blip to confirm →

Liquid, because the exit is the job

Getting in is easy. Getting out is the job. Helmsman does not hunt the penny name that «blows up tonight». It goes where the book still exists when you want to close — the curated universe. If the data is stale, the scan stops — it does not invent a price to keep you in the conversation.

Then, only on the names that stay lit, the risk engine does the numbers on your account. The scan does not size. The engine does. The AI, if present, does neither.
