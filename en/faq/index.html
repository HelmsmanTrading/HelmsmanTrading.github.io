Even straighter

Who executes — and whose risk? The broker. Helmsman is the pipe. The LLM can hallucinate. You confirm. We are not the final executor. See Disclaimer and Terms.

Does it manage the position after entry? No. It opened covered. The rest is your discipline. We do not sell overnight trailing.

Can I wire Directa or IBKR today? Not yet. They are on the map. Alpaca and Tradier have adapters. Write where you trade: that is the queue.

Are the logos on the slide a partnership? No. The marks name the venues. Gold on the tile = live bridge. Grey = map. No «certified integration» sticker.

Do I need a new account? No. The account stays yours. The bridge lengthens toward who you already use.
