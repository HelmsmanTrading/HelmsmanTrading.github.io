Even straighter

Who executes — and whose risk? The broker. Helmsman is the pipe. The LLM can hallucinate. You confirm. We are not the final executor. See Disclaimer and Terms.

Does it manage the position after entry? No. It opened covered. The rest is your discipline. We do not sell overnight trailing.

Can I wire Directa or IBKR today? IBKR has a mock-tested adapter; live is pending. Directa is integrable, but via Darwin on the machine — not today. Alpaca, Tradier and IG already have the bridge (paper/sandbox/demo). Fineco and DEGIRO have no retail trading API: they stay off the slide.

Are the logos on the slide a partnership? No. The marks name venues with an API that can be spoken. Gold = bridge already in code. Grey = to build. No official API, no marquee.

Do I need a new account? No. The account stays yours. The bridge lengthens toward who you already use.
