Covers

No naked entries. Period.

A market with no stop is not «fast». It is uncovered. Helmsman opens only if protection leaves with the entry. If the broker or the rule cannot cover, there is no order — even if you already said yes.

Two tapes, two fates

Red is the account that «adds the stop later». A spike, a gap, a headline: the line vanishes lower. Gold is the covered entry: the line breathes, the floor is there. It is not magic. It is the order class the venue accepts — bracket, OTO, OTOCO — not a SIMPLE market with a prayer in chat.

After the open Helmsman does not chase the stop for you. It did its job: open covered. Managing the position is your discipline. Anyone promising automatic trailing while you sleep is selling another product — and, usually, another risk you did not read.

The order class is the cover

You see size and stop before you confirm. L5 does not send a naked entry. On Alpaca it is OTO or a bracket. On Tradier it is OTOCO. On PaperSim the protective legs stay open on the bench. The name changes. The promise does not.

What «covered» means here

It means refusing. If the rule does not give a stop distance, if the venue will not take that order class, if the data is stale — Helmsman does not «make it work». Your capital is not an experiment to keep you in the conversation.

It also means honesty about afterwards. We do not sell position management. We do not sell trailing. We do not sell autopilot. We sell the entry that is born already protected, on the account you already keep, with your yes.
