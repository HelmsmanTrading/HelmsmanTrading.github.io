Vs the chat that «knows what to buy»

A model is good at talking. It is terrible at sizing. Helmsman keeps it out of lots and stops. It can help you read; it cannot sign the order. The gap between advice and a burned account is all there.

Vs signal channels

A signal is a ticker and a side, naked. You copy, naked. Helmsman is not a channel: if the rule does not fire, there is no line. If there is, it has size and stop on your account, not on the account in the screenshot.

Vs the bot that runs at night

Autopilot is brochure language. Helmsman is the opposite: without your yes, nothing leaves. After entry it does not «manage». Anyone who wants a robot that prints while they sleep is looking for another product — and, usually, finds it at the bottom of the account.

Vs another «all-in» broker

We do not move your book. Alpaca and Tradier already have adapters; IBKR, Directa, Fineco, Schwab are on the map. The yes stays the same. Who receives the covered order changes. See the map →

Vs the spreadsheet and three screens

Scan by eye, size by feeling, stop «later». It works until it does not. Helmsman does not take the craft away: it takes the circus away. The decision stays. It leaves only if it is covered.
