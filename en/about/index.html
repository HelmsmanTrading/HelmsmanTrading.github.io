Why it exists

Because the market is full of naked opinions. Because «I’ll add the stop next» is a sentence accounts remember. Because switching brokers should not mean relearning how to get burned. Because a language model is good at talking and terrible at sizing.

Helmsman is the bridge: scan on liquid names, numbers from the engine, your yes, a covered entry at the venue you connect. Today Alpaca paper and Tradier sandbox. Tomorrow the map. Live when it deserves it — not to close a deal.

A project by Antonio Trento. We are in early access: skills and adapters are moving fast. Paper, when there is a seat, is for clients.
