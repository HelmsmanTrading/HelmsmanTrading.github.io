About

The helm, not the autopilot.

A helmsman holds the wheel. Not the crew rowing at random, not the autopilot. In the product you set the course: Helmsman scans, proposes, covers, talks to the broker. The AI, if present, does not pick size and does not fire the order.

Built for traders who want a simpler day: liquid markets, covered positions, a human yes, the account you already keep. Not for people hunting another quant framework or a bot that «prints while you sleep».

A project by Antonio Trento. Early access is paper. Live when it deserves to be.
