If three broker errors land in a row, or session drawdown clears a threshold, the switch arms. Preview and confirm stop. You disarm it by clearing the file — not a chat that «tries again». Positions already open stay yours: the kill-switch is not a trailing stop, it is a halt.

Why this part is boring, and must stay boring

Low conviction: refuse. Too many positions: cap. Heat over the ceiling: no. Long-only skill: no invented shorts. Idempotency: no doubling the same name. The LLM does not compute lots. It does not compute the stop. It does not «nudge» size because the prompt was persuasive.

Early access is paper. Live comes when these vetoes still hold with real money — not before.
