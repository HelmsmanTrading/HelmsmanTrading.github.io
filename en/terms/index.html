These terms describe the software’s role as designed — including the SaaS architecture draft. They are not a substitute for legal advice. The owner may update them. Continued use is acceptance.

1. Bridge, not a mandate

Helmsman Trading is routing infrastructure (middleware / pass-through). It is not an investment adviser, not a licensed intermediary, not an asset manager, not an exchange. It does not originate the market call by itself: it translates, filters and forwards between (a) a third-party language model, (b) you, (c) the API of the broker you connect.

2. Who does what

You hold the account, the keys, the human confirm, the risk parameters, and the law of your country. The LLM (Anthropic, OpenAI or another client) can be wrong. The broker is the final executor: it accepts, rejects, delays, gaps. Helmsman is none of those three roles.

3. Hallucinations and software AS IS

Language models invent data, botch maths, and are not deterministic. Helmsman declines liability for losses born of a model’s misread, a badly invoked tool, or an «almost» the AI treated as a yes. The software is provided as is, with no warranty of uptime, latency, or freedom from bugs. A guardrail can fail. A network can lag. We do not become your refund.

4. Keys, risk, third parties

Broker API keys stay yours. We do not hold your book. You are responsible for issuing them with withdrawals disabled and tight limits. We depend on Anthropic, your client (Claude Code, Cursor, other), the venue, and hosting. If one of them dies in a crash, Helmsman does not flatten your book for you and does not answer for lost capital.

5. Paper and live

Access described today is paper. Live, if it comes, keeps the same frame: middleware, your yes, executor = broker. Nothing here is an offer of investment services.
