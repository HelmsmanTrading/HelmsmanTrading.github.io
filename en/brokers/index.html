An adapter is not a logo

Putting Interactive Brokers, Directa or Schwab on a page is an afternoon of CSS. Getting a stop-attached entry there is a contract, a payload, a session clock, a way to read the order list. Alpaca wants brackets and OTO, a helmsman_… client id. Tradier wants OTOCO in a form payload, a hyphenated tag, a bearer token. Idempotency, a closed cash session, how a stop is reconciled: all different.

Paste Alpaca onto Tradier and you open naked while calling the mess «multi-broker». Helmsman does not pretend the world is one API. Every adapter carries its own engagement card: how you enter, how you must not, what happens when the session is closed. You stay a client of whoever gives you the book. We lengthen the bridge, venue by venue.

In code today: Alpaca (paper) and Tradier (sandbox), plus PaperSim locally to prove the rules with the network off. IG is already stubbed in the factory. Interactive Brokers, Schwab, TradeStation, tastytrade, E*TRADE, Fidelity, Robinhood, Webull, Directa, Fineco, DEGIRO, Saxo, eToro, Plus500, Trading 212, Trade Republic, Swissquote, CMC: names traders actually use. They deserve their own adapter when contract and APIs hold — not a sticker.

The marks you see belong to their owners. They name the venues; they do not sell a partnership that does not exist. Gold on the tile = live bridge. Grey = on the map. No fake «certified integration».

Two real adapters. Two grammars.

The same human yes. The same cover. Different payloads. If the bridge does not respect the venue, it is not a bridge: it is a bug with a logo.

ADAPTER · PAPER

Alpaca

First live bridge. Brackets and OTO, a traceable client id, paper for early access. This is where we learned that «covered» is an order class, not a promise in chat.

  • — OTO / bracket, not SIMPLE
  • — Human confirm before submit
  • — Engagement rules on the adapter, not the LLM

ADAPTER · SANDBOX

Tradier

Second real broker. OTOCO in a form payload, a sanitised tag, sandbox. It proved the thesis: copy Alpaca here and you open naked. So we do not copy it.

  • — OTOCO, not a dressed-up bracket
  • — Tags without underscores
  • — The same preview → confirm loop

PaperSim stays the bench: no keys, no network, so the rules can fire before a venue is touched. It is not a broker. It is the workshop.

What we will not do

  • We will not move your book onto an omnibus account of ours. We are not your broker.
  • We will not sell a «partner» venue as the only way to use Helmsman.
  • We will not go live on a venue until covers and engagement rules are honest there.
  • We will not paste Alpaca’s rules onto everyone. Tradier already proved it: respect the venue, or lie.

What we ask

On the access form, name the broker you already use. It is not a pitch to open a new account. It is the queue: Directa, IBKR, Fineco, Schwab tell us where to lengthen the bridge after Alpaca and Tradier.

Your yes stays the same. Only who receives the covered order changes. That is the only invariant we are selling you.
