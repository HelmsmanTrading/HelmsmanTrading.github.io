Multi-broker

We are not asking for another account. We are asking for the bridge keys.

One idea: if a broker exposes an API, Helmsman should speak it. You stay a client of whoever gives you the book and the rulebook. We adapt the bridge, venue by venue — without pasting Alpaca’s rules onto everyone.

Today

Alpaca paper

Live bridge, early access, practice capital. This is where we measure scan, covers, and human confirm without burning real money.

Next

Every API that earns it

Interactive Brokers, other US names, European venues when the contract and the APIs hold. A new adapter brings its rules: sessions, order types, how a stop is read. No «works like Alpaca» stamped on top.

What we will not do

  • We will not move your book onto an omnibus account of ours.
  • We will not sell a «partner» broker as the only way to use Helmsman.
  • We will not go live on a venue until covers and engagement rules are honest there.

On the access form, tell us which broker you already use. That decides what we connect next — it is not a pitch for a new account.
