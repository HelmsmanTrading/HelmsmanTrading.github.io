System Architecture

Monolithic trading bots fail during high volatility. Helmsman Trading is built on a decentralized, decoupled 5-layer architecture designed for uncompromising resilience.

Layer 1

The Data Lake

Depending on REST APIs for every indicator calculation introduces network latency and rate-limit risks. The Helmsman L1 Data Lake natively ingests tick data from SIP providers (Polygon, IEX) and streams it into an ultra-fast local SQLite and memory cache.

  • Local VWAP & Moving Averages calculated at zero network cost.
  • Immediate Historical Replay for offline backtesting.

Layer 2

Decentralized Plugins

You shouldn't need to fork a framework to add a strategy. Helmsman uses a dynamic plugin registry. Just write a Python class, decorate it with @register_skill, and drop it in the folder. The engine will discover and run it in its own isolated sandbox.

Layer 4

The Risk Gatekeeper

It's not a matter of if your algorithmic strategy will make a mistake, but when. The L4 Risk Engine sits directly between your logic (L2) and the market (L5). It strictly enforces global maximum heat limits, dynamic stop-losses, and exposure rules.

If a strategy attempts to open a position that exceeds your defined risk tolerance, L4 will silently block the execution and alert you. Your capital remains safe.
