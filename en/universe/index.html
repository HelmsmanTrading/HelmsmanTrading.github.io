Universe

Fifty-two names. Chosen because you can exit. Not because they look dramatic.

Helmsman will not screen «the whole market». It scans a curated book of liquid names and ETFs — where the spread does not eat you, volume exists, and skills have real ground. The exit is the job. The universe is where that job is still possible.

Why not «every ticker on earth»

A product that promises to watch ten thousand symbols is not selling edge. It is selling noise. More names, more false starts, more temptation to «find something» when the day is empty. Helmsman does the opposite: it narrows the field to what earns a line.

The universe is not a runtime volume filter. It is a curation: tech megacaps, blue chips, liquid sectors, ETFs where institutional flow exists. The skills — trend, mean reversion, squeeze, quality dip, AVWAP — were built and validated on these reference markets. Not on a vanity dataset. Not on the thin name that «could 10×».

If a name is not in the book, Helmsman does not invent the trade to keep you in the conversation. If data is stale, it skips. If the rule does not fire, it skips. That sobriety is the product.

Liquidity = where skills work

Not every skill watches all 52. ATR-BREAKOUT, CONNORS-MR and VOL-SQUEEZE run the full book. QUALITY-DIP narrows to quality megacaps: a dip only makes sense if the name has an institutional floor, not a lottery ticket. AVWAP-RETEST narrows further: only where anchored VWAP means something — indexes, megacaps, liquid sectors. On thin names the line is not support. It is noise.

That is the commercial reason, said straight: skills are not «universal». They are built for reference markets. Validating them on a different universe would be theatre. Helmsman prefers a short catalog that fires where it should.

What the universe is not

Not a buy recommendation. Not personal advice. Not a partnership with Apple, JPMorgan or SPDR. Marks name venues and names — they certify nothing. Helmsman is middleware: it translates, vetoes, forwards. The final executor stays your broker, after your yes.

Not a signal channel. Not «52 trades a day». On most sessions many names stay dark. A WMT under the rule’s average does not become a trade because «Walmart is solid». It becomes a skip. That honesty is what you are buying.

Not closed forever. The book can lengthen when an adapter, a skill and the liquidity earn it. It does not lengthen to fill a slide.

From the book to the proposal

The scan walks the tickers. Skills answer yes/no. Only the yeses become a preview with size and stop. You confirm. The broker — Alpaca paper or Tradier sandbox today, the map later — gets the covered order. No size invented in chat. No naked entry with a promise to «add the stop later».

If you want ten thousand tickers and a bot that prints while you sleep, you are on the wrong site. If you want a liquid book, rules validated on reference markets, and a hand on the helm — you are in the right place.
