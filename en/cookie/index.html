What we do not do

This site does not set marketing, profiling, or first-party analytics cookies. There is no Google Analytics tag, no Meta pixel, no «accept analytics» switch that turns on something imaginary. A banner like that would be a lie.

Storage on your device

We use browser localStorage — not an HTTP cookie, still on your disk:

  • theme — light or dark, if you use the toggle.
  • cta_popup_closed — if you close the bottom-right card, we do not show it on every visit.

They keep the site as you left it. Clear them in the browser and the theme resets, the popup may return.

Third parties when the page loads

GitHub Pages (hosting), Google Fonts, Cloudflare cdnjs (Font Awesome), the Tailwind CDN, and Simple Icons (broker marks) answer the browser’s requests. They may set their own cookies or see an IP. We do not switch them on with a tracking script: they are theme assets. The access form, if you submit it, goes through FormSubmit — see Privacy.

Why there is no consent wall

EU law asks for informed consent for non-essential cookies. We do not install those. An «analytics / marketing» panel copied from another template would be theatre. If we add measurement or ads one day, this page and any banner will change first — not after.
