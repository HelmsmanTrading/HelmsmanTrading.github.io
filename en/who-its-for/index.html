Who it is for

For people who trade. Not people who collect tools.

Helmsman is not for everyone. If you want adrenaline, signals, or a robot that prints while you sleep, this page saves you an email. If you want three liquid proposals, real numbers, and an entry already covered, stay.

Four people you would recognise

SWING · FINITE TIME

Elena

She has a job. She cannot sit the book. She wants three liquid proposals at the end of the day, not fifty alerts. Helmsman takes the scan and leaves her the yes.

DISCIPLINE · STOPS

Davide

He knows «this time without a stop» is how accounts burn. He wants the entry born already covered, or not born. The rest is noise.

MULTI-BROKER

Sara

She already has a broker, maybe two. Directa and a US account. She does not want another terminal: she wants a bridge that speaks the APIs on offer.

AI, BUT NOT THE NUMBERS

Paolo

He already asks the AI «what would you do on SLV». He does not want it inventing lots. He wants a real line: size, stop, target — and his yes.

Who it is not for

Not for you ifyou want an overnight bot, copy naked signals, hunt the penny that «blows up tonight», or want the AI to sign size and stop.
For you ifyou trade liquid names, hate naked entries, keep the account you already have, and want to say yes to a line — not a circus.

We do not need to convince everyone. We need to convince the people who already know the problem is not «another opinion».
