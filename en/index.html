03. The Risk Engine

Your capital is not an experiment

In quantitative trading, a single bug in your strategy can wipe out your account. The Helmsman L4 Risk Engine isolates your code from the market, acting as an unyielding gatekeeper that enforces maximum heat and dynamic stop-losses globally.

Global Heat Limits

Define exactly how much capital can be at risk at any given moment. The broker bridge will outright reject orders that exceed your heat limit.

Local Data Resiliency

Don't rely on laggy external APIs for critical indicators. The L1 Data Lake consolidates Polygon and IEX data in a local SQLite DB for sub-millisecond VWAP lookups.

Plug-and-play Routing

Switch seamlessly between Alpaca, Tradier, and a local PaperSim engine without altering a single line of your L2 strategies.
