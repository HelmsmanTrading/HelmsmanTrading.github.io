Covered positions

No naked entries. Full stop.

A market order with no stop is not «fast»: it is uncovered. Helmsman opens only when the cover is there — a stop, and a target when the rule calls for one. If that broker cannot cover, it does not open.

  • You see size and stop before you confirm.
  • The numbers come from the risk engine, not from chat.
  • After entry, the position is yours: Helmsman does not «manage» it for you.

The loop

Four steps. No theatre.

01

Scan

Looks at liquid markets. Stale data or a rule that does not fire: skip. It does not invent the trade.

02

Propose

One line: symbol, side, size, stop, target. Enough to say yes or no — not a 40-page memo.

03

You confirm

Helmsman is the helmsman, not the captain. Without your yes, nothing is sent.

04

Open covered

The entry hits the broker you connected, already protected. Then the position is yours.
