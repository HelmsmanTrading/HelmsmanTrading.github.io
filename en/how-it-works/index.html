How it works

You decide. He runs the rules.

Helmsman is not a second brain that «knows the tape». It is a bridge: it watches liquid names, proposes only where a rule fires, computes size and stop, and talks to the broker. The yes stays yours.

1. Scan the liquid tape

It looks where getting in and out is realistic: names and ETFs with a real book. Stale data, a rule that does not fire, a «almost» setup — skip. An honest no beats an invented trade.

2. One line on the table

Symbol, side, size, stop, target. That is the preview: nothing has been sent. Size is not dictated by a chatbot. The risk engine computes it on your account, with caps and a fixed risk fraction.

3. You say yes — or you pass

Helmsman holds the helm; you are still captain. Without a human confirm, there is no order. If size moved or the rule no longer applies, it stops. There is no «just execute».

4. Open covered, at your broker

The entry hits the account you connected, already protected. After that the position is yours: Helmsman does not chase the stop for you, does not «manage» the trade on autopilot. You opened covered. The rest is your discipline.

The live bridge today is Alpaca paper. The same logic should hold for every broker with an API: venue rules on the table, no Alpaca copy pasted as universal law.

What it is not

  • Not a signal to copy with your eyes closed.
  • Not a bot that runs while you sleep.
  • Not a framework to clone so you can «write skills in Python» — that, if you need it, lives in a repo on the side.
