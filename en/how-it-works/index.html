Why the loop is short on purpose

You already have three screens, a Telegram channel, a spreadsheet, and a model that «would tell you what to do». The shortage is not opinions. It is that the opinion arrives naked: no size, no stop, on the wrong broker, after the tape has moved. Then you «add the stop next». Next never comes. The account knows.

Helmsman reverses the order. First it scans names and ETFs where getting in and out is realistic. Then, only where a deterministic rule fires, it computes size and stop on your account. Then it puts one line on the table. Then it waits for your yes. Only then it talks to the broker — Alpaca or Tradier today, the next venue when the adapter earns it.

It is not a second brain that «knows the market». It is middleware: a bridge with rules. The LLM, if present, can help you read context — and it can hallucinate. It cannot invent lots. It cannot fire the order. You are captain. The final executor is the broker on your account. Helmsman translates, vetoes, forwards — and refuses the course if the cover is missing.

This page is not a developer tutorial. It is the day you are buying: few moves, many vetoes, zero theatre. If you want a bot that prints while you sleep, you are on the wrong site — and, usually, hunting the wrong product.

The loop, visible

Every step has a veto. Stale data stops the scan. A rule that does not fire skips the name. Size that moved blocks confirm. A naked market never leaves.

01 · SCAN

Scan the liquid tape

Names and ETFs with a real book. Stale data, price under the rule’s average, an «almost» — skip. An honest no beats a setup invented to keep you glued.

02 · PREVIEW

One line, real numbers

Symbol, side, size, stop, target. Nothing has been sent. Size comes from the risk engine: a fixed fraction, caps, buying power. Not from a chat with «a good feeling on SLV».

03 · CONFIRM

Your yes, or nothing

Without a human confirm there is no order. If size moved or the rule no longer applies, it stops. No «just execute». No bot running while you sleep.

04 · COVERED

Opens, already protected

The entry hits the account you connected, with a stop (and a target if the rule calls for one). Then the position is yours. Helmsman does not «manage» it for you. It opened covered. The rest is discipline.

The scan is not a signal channel

The radar turns on liquid names. It lights only what deserves a line. A name under the rule’s average stays dark. Data older than the session threshold: skipped. A dead weekend tape does not become a «signal» to keep you awake.

The rules — ATR breakout, mean reversion, squeeze — watch the market, not your account. The account arrives later, when size is computed. Helmsman does not propose a long because «you have buying power». It proposes one if the tape says yes.

What you actually see

Not a developer log. Three proposals, one skipped. SLV fires the breakout: size, stop, target. V fires mean reversion, already covered. WMT sits under the average: not applicable. Nobody asks you to «force» WMT because you feel like trading.

That line is all you need to say yes or no. If you want the rule detail, it is there. If you want the decision, it is there. The site is not selling a framework to clone: it is selling the moment when the dirty work is done and the helm is still yours.

The vetoes are not a bug. They are the product.

Trading breaks when software «makes it work». Helmsman prefers a visible no to a naked yes. Stale data, a rule that does not fire, size that moved, a market with no stop: four closed doors. Behind each one is an account that did not become an anecdote.

If three broker errors land in a row, or session drawdown clears a threshold, the kill-switch arms. Preview and confirm stop. You disarm it. Positions already open stay yours: the halt is not a trailing stop, it is a brake.

The objections we hear. Straight answers.

«Do I need to code?» No. You see proposals, read size and stop, say yes or no. The stack stays under the hood. Helmsman is for people who trade, not people who write bots.

«Does it print while I sleep?» No. Without your yes nothing leaves. After entry it does not manage the position. Anyone hunting a night robot is looking for another product — and, usually, finds it at the bottom of the account.

«Do I have to switch brokers?» No. The account stays yours. Today the bridge speaks Alpaca, Tradier, IG and IBKR (the last one mock, live pending). Saxo, Schwab, Directa and the other retail APIs are to build. Fineco and DEGIRO have no official trading API: we will not fake the bridge. Pre-register and name where you already trade: that is the queue, not a pitch for a new account.
