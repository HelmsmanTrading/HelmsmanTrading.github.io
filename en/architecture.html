The Architecture

Built to withstand edge cases, flash crashes, and API failures. Not just a script, but a fortress.

L1: Data Lake

The foundation. Fetches and consolidates SIP data from Polygon and IEX, computing resilient session VWAPs and storing them locally in SQLite for fast historical replay.

L2: Plugin Skills

A decentralized strategy registry. You write Python files, decorate them with @register, and the system automatically discovers and loads them.

L4: Risk Engine

The heart of Helmsman. Enforces heat limits, manages stop-losses, and acts as an uncompromising gatekeeper between your strategies and the market.

L5: Broker Bridge

Executes trades across Alpaca, Tradier, and a local PaperSim for completely offline backtesting. Pydantic-enforced schemas guarantee API consistency.
