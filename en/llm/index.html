A model is not the bridge

Putting Claude or GPT on a landing page is an afternoon of CSS. Getting that brain to call scan_market, see a preview with size and stop, wait for your yes, and only then talk to the broker — that is a craft. Helmsman exists because models are good at talking and terrible at sizing.

Paste a chat onto a live account and you open naked while calling the mess «AI trading». Helmsman does not pretend the world is one brain API. Every client carries its own grammar: how tools are invoked, how the preview is shown, how the human yes is held. You stay captain. The model stays a guest. We lengthen the bridge — and only if that brain can wait.

In code today: Claude (Anthropic) and Claude Code are native. Cursor is a client that can seat Claude, GPT or another tool-capable model. Claude Desktop via MCP fits. GPT (OpenAI) wires. Gemini, Grok, Mistral, Ollama: integrable, with friction — tool-calling is not a birthright. A naked chat, an autopilot, a channel that «would tell you what to do»: they stay off the slide.

The marks you see belong to their owners. They name who can speak the bridge; they do not sell a partnership. Gold on the tile = native. Grey = speaks tools. Amber = with friction. No fake «model certification».

Two chairs. One helm.

The client is where you type. The model is who proposes. Helmsman is the pipe in between. Confuse the three and you sign a thesis you did not read.

CLIENT · THE TABLE

Where you sit

Claude Code, Cursor, Claude Desktop. The place you ask for a scan, read the preview, say yes or no. Without a client that speaks tools, Helmsman is a repo. With a client that fires alone, Helmsman closes the pipe.

  • — Visible tools, not magic in the background
  • — Preview before submit
  • — The yes stays yours, always

MODEL · THE GUEST

Who proposes

Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, Mistral, a local weight. It can read context. It can hallucinate a ticker. It can treat an «almost» as a yes. That is why it does not number lots and does not touch the broker. It proposes. Then it is quiet.

  • — Proposal, not size
  • — No order without confirm
  • — If it hallucinates, the veto holds

The model vendor (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, Mistral, the weight on Ollama) is not Helmsman. If that vendor dies, the bridge does not flatten your book for you. See the Disclaimer.

The loop, from brain to book

Four moves. The model sits only in the first. Skip the second, the third or the fourth and you are not using Helmsman. You are using a chat with an account attached.

01 · LLM

Proposes

Asks for the scan, reads context, may suggest a thesis. It does not compute lots. It does not pick the stop. If it invents a name, the skill says no.

02 · BRIDGE

Vetoes and numbers

The skill answers with a bool. The risk engine writes size and stop on your account. Stale data, a rule that does not fire, a naked market: the pipe closes.

03 · YOU

Say yes. Or nothing.

You see symbol, side, size, stop, target. Without confirm the order does not exist. If size moved in the meantime, it stops. You are captain.

04 · BROKER

Executes, covered

The final executor is the venue on your account. Alpaca, Tradier, IG, IBKR — or the next honest adapter. Not the model. Not Helmsman.

VETO ON THE MODEL

What the LLM cannot do. Ever.

Helmsman does not «align» the model. It cages it. Four noes we do not negotiate, whatever the mark on the tile.

  • NO
    Invent the lots

    Size and stops come from the risk engine. A chat that «feels» 200 shares of SLV is a bug, not a feature.

  • NO
    Fire the order

    Without your yes the pipe is closed. Autopilot is not in the queue. It is the opposite of the product.

  • NO
    Treat an almost as a yes

    The skill is a bool. If it does not fire, no preview is born. The LLM cannot «force» a thesis the tape does not have.

  • NO
    Open naked

    Even if the model is sure. Cover or nothing. The order class is spoken by the adapter, not by prose.

Why Claude is native. Why the others can sit.

Helmsman was born on Claude Code: tools, a session, human confirm, the same table. That is not an endorsement. It is a fact of grammar. The veto was written against a model that can call functions and can (if you force it) wait.

Cursor is the second table because it does the same thing: agent, MCP, a model that can be Claude or GPT. The bridge does not change. Who sits does. GPT wires because the tool-calling is there. Gemini, Grok, Mistral, a Llama on Ollama: the craft is the same, the discipline is not. That is why they sit in the friction queue, not under an «AI-ready» sticker.

If your client does not expose tools, there is no Helmsman. There is a conversation. Conversations do not open covered positions.

CLAUDE CODE

Scan, preview, confirm, submit in one session. This is where the veto learned to speak. If you already use Claude Code, we are not asking for another IDE. We are asking for the yes on the line.

CURSOR · MCP

The same bridge, another editor

Cursor can seat Claude or GPT. Claude Desktop speaks MCP. Helmsman does not live «inside» the editor. The editor calls the bridge. If the editor fires alone, the bridge says no.

LOCAL · OLLAMA

No network, with discipline

A weight on the machine is not more honest than Claude. It is closer. Tool-calling must be proven, not assumed. If the local model does not wait for the yes, it is a bot. Bots do not pass.

What stays off the slide

Helmsman does not certify brains and does not sell a «partner model». If tool-calling is missing, or if the craft is firing while you sleep, the name does not run on the marquee.

What we will not do

  • We are not Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or xAI. We do not host their weights. We do not answer for their hallucinations.
  • We will not let the model pick size. The risk engine numbers. Period.
  • We will not open an autopilot «because the model is good». Prose skill is not an order class.
  • We will not sell an LLM logo as a certified integration. Gold = native. The rest is an honest map.

What we ask

On the pre-registration, name the client you already use — Claude Code, Cursor, other — and, if you want, the model. It is not a pitch to switch brains. It is the queue: whoever sits on GPT or a local weight tells us where to lengthen the bridge after Claude.

Your yes stays the same. Only who proposes the thesis changes — and only if that who speaks tools. That is the only invariant we are selling you on the model side. On the venue side, the map is the other shore.
