Privacy

This marketing site does not run your trading account. If you request paper access, we receive the email and optional notes you submit (broker, message) in order to contact you. We do not sell that list.

Connecting a broker happens in the product, with credentials you control. Strategy data is not harvested by this website.

The waitlist form is processed by FormSubmit and delivered to {{ site.contact_email }}. You can write the same address instead of using the form.
